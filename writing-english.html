📘 Good writing starts with a clear purpose

Innan du skriver behöver du veta vad du ska skriva och vem som ska läsa.

Purpose

Inform, describe, tell a story, explain or communicate.

Reader

Friend, teacher, class, website visitor or another audience.

Text type

Message, story, fact text, email or description.

När du vet syfte och mottagare blir det lättare att välja ord, ton och struktur.

🧠 1. Plan your ideas

Topic

What am I writing about?

Key words

Write useful words before full sentences.

Order

What comes first, next and last?

Reader

Who will read the text?

🔤 2. Build clear sentences

Simple sentence

I play football.

Add detail

I play football after school.

Add a reason

I play football after school because I enjoy being active.

🧱 3. Build paragraphs

Ett stycke samlar meningar som handlar om samma huvudidé.

Example paragraph

My favourite place is the beach near my grandparents' house. I like it because it is quiet and beautiful. In summer, I swim there almost every day. Sometimes we stay until sunset and eat dinner outside.
All sentences support one main idea: the writer's favourite place.

🔗 Linking words

Sambandsord hjälper meningar och stycken att hänga ihop.

Order

first, then, next, finally

Add

and, also, besides

Reason

because, so

Contrast

but, however

📱 Messages and emails

Example email

Hi Sam,

Thanks for your message. I'm free on Saturday afternoon. Would you like to meet at the sports centre at two o'clock? We could play basketball and get something to eat afterwards.

See you,
Alex

📖 Story writing

Beginning

Who? Where? When?

Problem

What happens?

Development

What do the characters do?

Ending

How does it finish?

Useful story words: suddenly, later, after that, while, eventually, finally.

📚 Fact writing

Title

Tell the reader what the text is about.

Paragraphs

Organise facts into clear parts.

Topic words

Use accurate vocabulary for the subject.

🎨 Descriptive writing

Gör texten tydligare med detaljer.

See

bright lights, dark clouds, a narrow street

Hear

loud music, birds singing, traffic

Feel

cold wind, warm sun, nervous, excited

Accuracy

⚠️ Common mistakes

Writing without a plan

❌ Börja direkt och hoppas att texten hänger ihop.

✅ Skriv några stödord och bestäm ordningen först.

Repeating the same starter

❌ I… I… I… I…

✅ Variera med time words, linking words och olika meningsstarter.

Only checking spelling

❌ Om orden är rättstavade är texten färdig.

✅ Kontrollera också innehåll, struktur, verb och tydlighet.

📝 Writing tasks

Planera först, skriv sedan och förbättra till sist.

📧 Task 1: Email to a friend

Write an email inviting a friend to do something at the weekend. Include time, place, activity and one question.


Hi …

Are you free on Saturday? Would you like to meet at the sports centre at two o'clock? We could play basketball and get something to eat afterwards. What do you think?

See you,
…

📖 Task 2: A surprising afternoon

Write a short story that starts with: “Everything seemed normal until I opened the door.”


Beginning: Where are you and who is there?
Problem: What surprising thing happens?
Development: What do you do?
Ending: How does the situation end?
Useful words: suddenly, then, while, after that, finally.

🌍 Task 3: Fact text

Write a short fact text about an animal, place or country. Use a title, two short paragraphs and at least three topic words.


Title
Paragraph 1: What is it? Where is it found?
Paragraph 2: Important facts, features or behaviour.
Use accurate topic words and linking words such as also, because and however.

🌱 Stöd och utmaning

🆘 I need support

  • Write key words before sentences.
  • Use a model text.
  • Use sentence starters.
  • Write one paragraph at a time.
  • Check one thing at a time: content, verbs, spelling, punctuation.

🚀 I want a challenge

  • Use varied sentence starters.
  • Replace basic words with more precise vocabulary.
  • Use longer sentences with because, when, while or although.
  • Edit your first draft to improve both language and structure.
