👋 Greetings – hälsa

Olika hälsningar passar i olika situationer.

Hello!

Hej!

Hi!

Hej! – vardagligt.

Good morning!

God morgon!

Good afternoon!

God eftermiddag!

I vardaglig engelska är Hi! och Hello! mycket vanliga.

🙂 How are you?

Vanliga svar behöver inte vara långa.

How are you?

I'm fine, thanks.

How's it going?

Pretty good, thanks.

How are you doing?

I'm okay. How about you?

Ett naturligt samtal fortsätter ofta med How about you? eller And you?

🙋 Introduce yourself

Presentera dig med några enkla meningar.

Example

Hello! My name is Alex.
I'm twelve years old.
I live in Sweden.
I like football and music.
My favourite subject is science.

❓ Ask questions

Frågor hjälper dig att hålla igång ett samtal.

What?

Vad?

Where?

Var?

When?

När?

Why?

Varför?

Who?

Vem?

How?

Hur?

Which?

Vilken/vilket?

How much / many?

Hur mycket / många?

🆘 Ask for help

Viktiga fraser i klassrummet och vardagen.

Can you help me, please?

Kan du hjälpa mig?

Can you repeat that, please?

Kan du säga det igen?

What does this word mean?

Vad betyder det här ordet?

How do you say … in English?

Hur säger man … på engelska?

I don't understand.

Jag förstår inte.

Could you speak more slowly?

Kan du prata långsammare?

🏫 At school

Vanliga meningar i klassrummet.

What page are we on?

Vilken sida är vi på?

Can I borrow a pencil?

Kan jag låna en penna?

I'm finished.

Jag är klar.

I need more time.

Jag behöver mer tid.

Can I work with a partner?

Kan jag arbeta med en partner?

What are we supposed to do?

Vad ska vi göra?

🕒 Talk about your day

Example

I get up at seven o'clock.
I have breakfast and go to school.
School starts at eight thirty.
After school, I usually meet my friends.
In the evening, I do my homework and relax.

Berätta vad du tycker om.

🍽️ At a café or restaurant

Mini dialogue

Customer: Hi. Can I have a sandwich, please?
Staff: Sure. Anything to drink?
Customer: Yes, a glass of water, please.
Staff: Of course.
Customer: Thank you.

Berätta mer.

⚠️ Common mistakes

“I am agree”

❌ I am agree.

✅ I agree.

“How are you?” – bara “Fine”

❌ Fine. [samtalet dör]

✅ I'm fine, thanks. How about you?

Glömma please

❌ Give me a pencil.

✅ Can I borrow a pencil, please?

💬 Speaking tasks

Öva gärna i par.

🙋 Task 1: Meet someone new

Ni träffas för första gången. Hälsa, presentera er, fråga om ålder, fritid och favoritämne. Ställ minst två följdfrågor.


A: Hi! My name is Sam. What's your name?
B: I'm Lina. Nice to meet you.
A: Nice to meet you too. What do you like doing in your free time?
B: I like swimming.
A: Really? How often do you swim?

🍽️ Task 2: At a café

En elev är kund och en är personal. Beställ något att äta och dricka, fråga om pris och avsluta artigt.


Customer: Hi. Can I have a cheese sandwich, please?
Staff: Of course. Anything to drink?
Customer: A glass of water, please. How much is it?
Staff: It's six pounds altogether.
Customer: Thank you.

🌱 Stöd och utmaning

🆘 I need support

  • Use short sentences.
  • Use question starters: What, Where, When, Why, How.
  • Keep useful phrases visible.
  • Practise with a partner first.
  • It is okay to ask: “Can you repeat, please?”

🚀 I want a challenge

  • Speak for two minutes without Swedish.
  • Ask at least three follow-up questions.
  • Use more natural reactions: Really? That's interesting! Me too!
  • Change the dialogue for a new situation.
