📘 Speaking is communication

Målet är inte perfekt engelska. Målet är att andra ska förstå vad du menar.

Say something

Våga använda språket även om du inte är säker på varje ord.

Listen and respond

Visa att du lyssnar och svara på det den andra personen säger.

Keep going

Använd följdfrågor och strategier när samtalet stannar upp.

Det är helt normalt att pausa, tänka om och formulera sig på nytt när man talar ett annat språk.

🛠️ Communication strategies

Du kan lösa problemet även om du saknar ett ord.

Describe it

“It's something you use for …”

Give an example

“For example …”

Use a simpler word

Byt till ett ord du kan.

Ask for help

“How do you say … in English?”

🧠 Think aloud when needed

Små fraser ger dig tid att tänka.

Let me think …

Well …

I'm not sure, but …

I mean …

🗣️ Pronunciation

Tydligt uttal handlar mer om förståelighet än om att låta exakt som en modersmålstalare.

Word stress

Vissa stavelser betonas mer: TAble, beGIN.

Sentence stress

Viktiga ord får mer betoning: “I LOVE that film.”

Pauses

Korta pauser gör talet tydligare och ger dig tid att tänka.

🎯 Give your opinion

I think …

I think school uniforms are useful.

In my opinion …

In my opinion, homework should be shorter.

I agree because …

I agree because it saves time.

I disagree because …

I disagree because students need choice.

📚 Describe and compare

Describe a person

She is friendly, patient and funny.

Describe a place

It is a small town near the sea.

Compare

London is bigger than my town, but my town is quieter.

💬 Model conversation

Topic: Free time

A: What do you like doing after school?
B: I usually play basketball or listen to music.
A: Really? How often do you play basketball?
B: About three times a week. What about you?
A: I don't play basketball, but I go swimming on Fridays.
B: Nice! Who do you go swimming with?

⚠️ Common mistakes

One-word answers

❌ “Yes.” “No.” “Fine.”

✅ Lägg till information: “Yes, I do. I play twice a week.”

Stopping for one missing word

❌ Samtalet tar slut.

✅ Beskriv ordet, använd exempel eller fråga om hjälp.

Perfect grammar first

❌ Jag vågar inte tala förrän allt är korrekt.

✅ Tydlig kommunikation kommer först. Språket förbättras genom användning.

🗣️ Speaking tasks

Öva i par eller små grupper.

🎯 Task 1: Two-minute conversation

Choose one topic: hobbies, school, food, travel or weekend plans. Speak for two minutes without using Swedish.


Start: What do you think about …?
Continue: Really? Why? How often? What about you?
Repair: Let me think … / How do you say … in English? / It's something you use for …

🧩 Task 2: Describe the mystery word

Choose an everyday object. Describe it without saying its name. Your partner guesses the word.


“It is something you carry when it rains. You hold it above your head. It helps you stay dry.” → umbrella

⚖️ Task 3: Agree or disagree

Discuss: “Students should have no homework.” Give an opinion, a reason, and ask your partner a follow-up question.


“I partly agree because students need time to rest. However, a small amount of homework can be useful. What do you think?”

🌱 Stöd och utmaning

🆘 I need support

  • Use sentence starters.
  • Prepare 5 key words before speaking.
  • Use short but complete answers.
  • Ask: “Can you repeat, please?”
  • Practise once before speaking to the class.

🚀 I want a challenge

  • Speak for three minutes without notes.
  • Ask at least four follow-up questions.
  • Use a repair strategy instead of Swedish when you forget a word.
  • Use opinions, reasons, examples and comparisons in the same conversation.
