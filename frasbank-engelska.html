🧠 Stöd för elever

🌱 När du behöver stöd

Välj 3–5 fraser från ett område och använd samma fraser flera gånger tills de känns naturliga.

🚀 När du vill utveckla språket

Byt ut enkla fraser mot mer utvecklade: “I think” → “In my opinion” → “One reason is that …”

🧠 Flashcards

Träna en fras i taget.

✍️ Träna fraser i sammanhang

1. Samtal

Skriv ett kort samtal med minst fyra fraser från frasbanken.


A: What do you think about school uniforms?
B: In my opinion, they can be useful.
A: Really? Why?
B: One reason is that everyone wears similar clothes. What about you?

2. Textrespons

Använd fraserna “The text is mainly about …”, “One clue is …” och “I think … because …”.


The text is mainly about friendship. One clue is that the characters help each other. I think the message is that good friends support each other because they stay together when things become difficult.

3. Presentation

Skriv en mini-inledning och ett avslut till en muntlig presentation.


Today I'm going to talk about Australia. First, I will explain where it is and then I will talk about wildlife. To finish, I will share one interesting fact. Do you have any questions?
