📘 What is a verb?

Verb beskriver handlingar, händelser eller tillstånd.

Action

run, write, play, eat

Event

happen, begin, change

State

be, know, like, understand

Verb ändrar ofta form beroende på vem som gör något och när det händer.

🔁 Present simple

Används ofta för vanor, rutiner och sådant som brukar vara sant.

I/You/We/They play.

Grundformen används oftast.

He/She/It plays.

Lägg ofta till -s.

Every day / usually / often

Vanliga tidsord.

Examples

I walk to school every day.

She plays tennis on Fridays.

They usually eat dinner at six.

❌ Negatives in present simple

I/You/We/They

do not / don't + verb

I don't like coffee.

He/She/It

does not / doesn't + verb

He doesn't play football.

Efter doesn't används grundformen: He doesn't play, inte “doesn't plays”.

❓ Questions in present simple

Do + I/you/we/they …?

Do you like music?

Do they live here?

Does + he/she/it …?

Does she speak English?

Does he walk to school?

⏳ Present continuous

Används ofta för något som händer just nu eller omkring nu.

I am + verb-ing

I am reading.

He/She/It is + verb-ing

She is sleeping.

You/We/They are + verb-ing

They are playing.

Examples

Look! It is raining.

We are working on a project right now.

Tom is talking to his teacher.

🔁 Present simple or present continuous?

Present simplePresent continuous
I play football every Tuesday.I am playing football now.
She usually walks to school.She is taking the bus today.
They live in London.They are staying in Paris this week.

🕒 Past simple

Används för avslutade händelser i det förflutna.

Regular verbs

play → played
walk → walked
visit → visited

Time words

yesterday, last week, last year, two days ago

Finished action

We visited our grandparents yesterday.

⚡ Irregular verbs

Vissa verb får en helt annan form i past simple.

Base formPast simpleExample
gowentWe went home.
seesawI saw a fox.
eatateShe ate breakfast.
havehadThey had fun.
dodidHe did his homework.
comecameMy friend came early.
taketookI took the bus.
getgotWe got home late.

❌ Negatives in past simple

did not / didn't + base form

I didn't go to school yesterday.

She didn't see the message.

They didn't play football.

Efter didn't används grundformen: didn't go, inte “didn't went”.

❓ Questions in past simple

Did you …?

Did you watch the film?

Where did …?

Where did they go?

What did …?

What did she eat?

🧠 Time clues

Tidsord kan hjälpa dig välja rätt tempus.

Present simple

always, often, usually, sometimes, every day

Present continuous

now, right now, at the moment, today

Past simple

yesterday, last week, ago, in 2024

❌ We didn't went home.

📝 Use the tenses

Skriv med flera tempus och visa att du kan skilja dem åt.

🔁 Task 1: My routine

Write 5 sentences about what you usually do on a school day. Use present simple.


I get up at seven. I usually eat breakfast at home. I walk to school. School starts at eight thirty. I do my homework in the afternoon.

⏳ Task 2: Right now

Write 4 sentences about what people are doing right now. Use present continuous.


My teacher is talking. Two students are writing. Someone is opening a book. I am working on this task.

🕒 Task 3: Yesterday

Write 5 sentences about yesterday. Use at least two irregular past forms.


Yesterday I went to school. I saw my friends. We had lunch together. After school, I walked home and did my homework.
