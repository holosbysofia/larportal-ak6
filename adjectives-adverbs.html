📘 What is an adjective?

Adjectives beskriver substantiv – personer, platser, saker och idéer.

a tall boy

tall beskriver boy

a red car

red beskriver car

an interesting book

interesting beskriver book

a difficult task

difficult beskriver task

På engelska står adjektivet ofta före substantivet: a small house.

🏃 What is an adverb?

Adverbs beskriver ofta hur, när, var eller hur mycket något händer.

How?

She speaks clearly.

When?

We arrived early.

Where?

Come inside.

How much?

It is very cold.

🧩 Adjective or adverb?

AdjectiveAdverbExample
carefulcarefullyShe is careful. / She works carefully.
slowslowlyThe car is slow. / The car moves slowly.
quietquietlyThe room is quiet. / He speaks quietly.
easyeasilyThe task is easy. / She solves it easily.
Många adverb bildas med -ly, men inte alla. Exempel: fast, hard, early.

↔️ Comparative – jämföra två

Använd comparative när du jämför två saker eller personer.

Short adjective + -er

small → smaller
fast → faster

y → -ier

happy → happier
easy → easier

more + adjective

interesting → more interesting
expensive → more expensive

Comparative används ofta med than: My bike is faster than yours.

🏆 Superlative – mest av tre eller fler

Short adjective + -est

small → smallest
fast → fastest

y → -iest

happy → happiest
easy → easiest

most + adjective

interesting → most interesting
expensive → most expensive

Superlative används ofta med the: the tallest, the most interesting.

🎯 Where do adverbs go?

Manner

She answered politely.

Frequency

I usually walk to school.

With be

He is always friendly.

📝 Description example

Basic

The dog was big. It ran fast.

Improved

The enormous brown dog ran quickly across the wet field.

Adjectives describe the dog and field. The adverb describes how it ran.

⚠️ Common mistakes

He runs quick

❌ He runs quick.

✅ He runs quickly.

more faster

❌ This car is more faster.

✅ This car is faster.

the most tallest

❌ She is the most tallest.

✅ She is the tallest.

📝 Use descriptive language

🎨 Task 1: Describe a person

Describe a person using at least five adjectives and one adverb.


My friend is cheerful, patient, creative, friendly and energetic. She speaks confidently and always helps other people.

↔️ Task 2: Compare two places

Compare two towns, countries, rooms or places. Use at least three comparative forms.


City A is bigger and busier than City B. City B is quieter and cheaper, but City A has more interesting museums.

🏆 Task 3: The best

Write four sentences using superlatives about your class, town, hobbies or favourite things.


Summer is the warmest season. Football is the most exciting sport for me. Saturday is the best day of the week. My local park is the quietest place near my home.

🌱 Stöd och utmaning

🆘 I need support

  • Ask: Does the word describe a noun or an action?
  • Use -ly for many adverbs.
  • Use -er/-est with many short adjectives.
  • Use more/most with many longer adjectives.
  • Keep a list of irregular forms: good–better–best.

🚀 I want a challenge

  • Replace simple adjectives with more precise ones.
  • Use comparative and superlative forms in the same paragraph.
  • Use adverbs of manner, time and frequency.
  • Edit a basic description to make it more vivid.
