Hur ofta?

🔁 Questions with do and does

Do + I/you/we/they …?

Do you like pizza?

Do they play football?

Does + he/she/it …?

Does she live here?

Does he speak English?

Efter does används verbets grundform: Does she like …?

🕒 Questions with did

Did används ofta när du frågar om något i dåtid.

Did you …?

Did you watch the film?

Where did …?

Where did they go?

What did …?

What did she eat?

Efter did används grundformen: Did you go? Inte “Did you went?”

✅ Short answers

Korta svar kan vara naturliga om de byggs rätt.

Do you like maths?

Yes, I do.
No, I don't.

Does she play tennis?

Yes, she does.
No, she doesn't.

Did they go home?

Yes, they did.
No, they didn't.

Are you ready?

Yes, I am.
No, I'm not.

💬 Better answers

Utveckla svaren med en detalj eller orsak.

Example

A: Do you like reading?
B: Yes, I do. I usually read adventure books.
A: Why do you like them?
B: Because they are exciting and unpredictable.

🔄 Follow-up questions

Följdfrågor bygger vidare på det någon redan har sagt.

Why?

Why do you like it?

How often?

Who with?

What happened next?

🧠 Open and closed questions

Closed question

Kan ofta besvaras med ja eller nej.

Do you like football?

Open question

Kräver oftast ett längre svar.

Why do you like football?

Am I late?

💬 Conversation tasks

🎯 Task 1: Ask five questions

Choose a topic: school, hobbies, travel or food. Write five different questions, including at least two open questions.


What is your favourite subject?
Why do you like it?
Do you have homework today?
How often do you study at home?
Who do you usually work with?

🔄 Task 2: Keep the conversation going

Write a short dialogue with at least three questions and three answers.


A: What do you like doing after school?
B: I like playing basketball.
A: How often do you play?
B: About twice a week.
A: Who do you play with?
B: Usually my friends from school.

🌱 Stöd och utmaning

🆘 I need support

  • Start with a question word.
  • Use Do/Does/Did when needed.
  • Remember the base verb after does and did.
  • Use short answers: Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
  • Add one follow-up question.

🚀 I want a challenge

  • Use both open and closed questions.
  • Ask follow-up questions based on the other person's answer.
  • Use polite questions naturally.
  • Write a dialogue that changes between present and past questions.
