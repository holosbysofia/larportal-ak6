👀 Skimming and scanning

Skimming

Läs snabbt för att förstå huvudidén och textens upplägg.

Bra när du vill veta: What is this text mainly about?

Scanning

Leta snabbt efter en särskild uppgift.

Bra när du söker till exempel ett datum, namn eller pris.

🎯 Main idea and details

Skilj det viktigaste från detaljer.

Example text

Many students ride bicycles to school. Cycling is cheap, gives exercise and does not produce exhaust fumes. However, cyclists need safe roads and should always use a helmet.

Main idea: Cycling to school has several advantages but safety is important.

🧠 Infer – read between the lines

Ibland står svaret inte direkt i texten.

Example

Leo looked out of the window. Dark clouds covered the sky. He put his football boots back in the cupboard and reached for a board game.

Inference: Leo probably thinks the weather will stop him from playing football outside.

📝 Summarise

Sammanfatta med det viktigaste – inte alla detaljer.

1. Find the topic

Vad handlar texten om?

2. Choose key points

Vilka 2–4 saker är viktigast?

3. Use your own words

Skriv kort med egna formuleringar.

📖 Reading text: The missing phone

On Tuesday afternoon, Maya stayed late at school to finish a group project. When she finally packed her bag, she noticed that her phone was missing. She checked under the table, inside her jacket and even in the recycling bin. Nothing.

Then she remembered using the phone in the library before lunch. She hurried upstairs, but the library was already closed. Maya sighed and wrote a note for the librarian.

The next morning, the librarian smiled when Maya entered. “Looking for this?” she asked, holding up the phone. Someone had found it near the computers and handed it in.

Think: Where did Maya probably lose her phone? Why did she write a note? What does the librarian's smile tell you?

⚠️ Common mistakes

Translate every word

❌ Jag kan inte gå vidare förrän varje ord är översatt.

✅ Försök först förstå huvudidén och använd sammanhanget.

Guess without evidence

❌ En inferens är vad jag själv tycker.

✅ En inferens måste bygga på ledtrådar i texten.

Summary = copy

❌ Jag kopierar hela stycken.

✅ En sammanfattning väljer ut det viktigaste och använder egna ord.

🌧️ Text 1

Mia opened the front door and immediately stepped back. Rain was falling heavily and the wind pushed the trees from side to side. She looked at the picnic basket in her hand and laughed.

Question: Why does Mia laugh?


She probably laughs because the weather is terrible for a picnic. The rain, wind and picnic basket are the clues.

🐕 Text 2

Every afternoon, Ben's dog waits by the window at four o'clock. At 4:10, its tail starts moving. A few minutes later, Ben's school bus stops outside the house.

Question: What has the dog learned?


The dog has learned when Ben usually comes home. It waits before the bus arrives because it recognises the daily routine.

🌱 Stöd och utmaning

🆘 I need support

  • Read the title first.
  • Underline words you know.
  • Look for names, times and places.
  • Do not stop at every unknown word.
  • Use the sentence before and after for clues.

🚀 I want a challenge

  • Explain answers with evidence from the text.
  • Write a short summary without copying.
  • Compare two characters or viewpoints.
  • Identify information that is stated directly and information that must be inferred.
