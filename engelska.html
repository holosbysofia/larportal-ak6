🗣️ Useful classroom English

Fraser som hjälper dig att använda engelska även när språket känns svårt.

Can you repeat, please? What does … mean? How do you say … in English? I think … I agree because … I don't understand yet. Could you help me? In my opinion … For example … First … then … finally …
