📘 Basic word order

Engelska har ofta en tydlig grundordföljd.

Subject + verb + information
Emma→ plays→ football after school.
På engelska behöver en vanlig mening nästan alltid ett tydligt subjekt.

👤 Subject

Subjektet berättar vem eller vad meningen handlar om.

I

I read every evening.

You

You speak English.

He / She / It

She likes music.

We / They

They live in London.

📍 Place and time

Plats kommer ofta före tid i en vanlig engelsk mening.

Subject + verb + place + time
We→ play football→ in the park→ on Saturdays.
Det finns variationer, men detta är ett bra grundmönster för åk 6.

❓ Questions with do and does

Do + subject + verb …?

Do you like football?

Do they live here?

Does + subject + verb …?

Does she like football?

Does he live here?

Efter does används verbets grundform: Does she play? Inte “Does she plays?”

❓ Question words

What

What do you want?

Where

Where do you live?

When

When does school start?

Why

Why do you like it?

Who

Who is your teacher?

How

How do you get to school?

Which

Which book do you prefer?

How often

How often do you train?

❌ Negative sentences

don't

I don't play tennis.

They don't understand.

doesn't

She doesn't like coffee.

He doesn't walk to school.

isn't / aren't

He isn't tired.

They aren't at home.

🔄 Word order with adverbs

Ord som always, usually, often, sometimes och never placeras ofta före huvudverbet.

Before the main verb

I usually eat breakfast at seven.

Before the main verb

She often walks to school.

After be

He is always friendly.

🧱 Longer sentences

Du kan koppla ihop idéer med vanliga sambandsord.

and

I like music and I play the guitar.

but

I like football, but I don't like running.

because

I study English because I want to travel.

so

It was raining, so we stayed inside.

📌 There is / There are

There is

There is a book on the table.

En sak.

There are

There are three books on the table.

Flera saker.

⚠️ Common mistakes

Wrong question order

❌ Where you live?

✅ Where do you live?

Wrong verb after does

❌ Does she likes football?

✅ Does she like football?

Missing subject

❌ Is raining.

✅ It is raining.

📝 Build and improve sentences

🧩 Task 1: Build a sentence

Write one sentence using this pattern: subject + verb + place + time.


My sister studies English at home every evening.

❓ Task 2: Turn statements into questions

Change these into questions: “You like music.” “She lives in London.” “They play football.”


Do you like music?
Does she live in London?
Do they play football?

🔧 Task 3: Improve the text

Correct the word order: “My brother every day plays after school basketball.”


My brother plays basketball after school every day.

🌱 Stöd och utmaning

🆘 I need support

  • Find the subject first.
  • Then find the verb.
  • Use Do/Does before the subject in many present simple questions.
  • Remember: after does, use the base verb.
  • Use sentence frames when needed.

🚀 I want a challenge

  • Write questions with several different question words.
  • Use subject and object pronouns correctly in one paragraph.
  • Vary where time expressions appear when the sentence still sounds natural.
  • Combine ideas with and, but, because and so.
